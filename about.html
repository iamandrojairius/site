About

The operator behind the brands.

Andro Jairius Kuissode — founder and managing member of MARK VII-XXXIV LLC.

In short

E-commerce operator since 2023. Media buyer and funnel builder. Founder of MARK VII-XXXIV LLC and of the Dgtl Ops brand. Based in Cotonou, Benin, working toward international markets.

Why I started

I entered e-commerce in 2023 with one conviction: selling online is a real trade, not a shortcut. Dropshipping was my entry point, and I never wanted another path. Building a store, finding a product, writing an offer, buying traffic, answering customers — each of these is a skill, and together they make a business.

What it cost me to learn

Like many people, I paid for courses and coaching. Most of them taught the visible part — the product, the store, the ad — and skipped what actually decides whether a business can operate at all: a properly registered company, payment processing that does not get shut down, policies that platforms accept, and the patience to let a test run long enough to mean something.

I learned those parts the hard way, on my own accounts. That is why the company exists in the form it has today.

What I do now

Through MARK VII-XXXIV LLC, I operate e-commerce brands for the US and English-speaking markets, run paid acquisition on Meta and TikTok, and design the stores and funnels behind them. Under the Dgtl Ops brand, I publish practical guides so that others can set up the same foundations without paying for the same mistakes.

Where this is going

The goal is simple to state: to become a reference in digital marketing and e-commerce — someone whose methods are trusted because they are real. In the longer term, that includes building tools and infrastructure that make it easier for African entrepreneurs to sell to the world.
